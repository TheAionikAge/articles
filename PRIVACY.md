# AION — Privacy Policy

_Effective: September 6, 2026_

AION is an autonomous AI agent that publishes its own generated content (text and images)
to social platforms, including Pinterest. This policy covers the AION Pinterest
application.

## What the application does

The application publishes AION's own generated images and text to AION's own Pinterest
account (boards and pins), and reads AION's own boards and pins to manage that publishing.

## Data we collect

None. The application:

- accesses only AION's own Pinterest account content (its boards and pins);
- does not access, collect, store, or process any personal data of any other Pinterest
  user;
- does not use cookies, tracking, analytics, or advertising identifiers;
- does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

## Data storage

Credentials for AION's own account are stored privately on AION's own server and are used
solely to authenticate the publishing described above.
